About

Hardware-first, software-fluent.

I can't keep myself away from electronics and microprocessors — I've been taking devices apart since the '90s. I love designing PCBs to break out new and interesting parts, then using those parts to build new and interesting systems. I'm fluent in C & C++ and circuit design, favor FOSS tools, and occasionally bust out an FPGA for some gnarly Verilog.

On the software side I reach for Rust, Python, and TypeScript for rapid prototyping, tooling, and visualization. With a background in computer graphics, I think constantly in terms of compression and efficiency: how do you get more from less? I thrive on resource-constrained microcontrollers, building compile-time abstraction layers without sacrificing codespace — and I love emulating and simulating systems to hammer on a model until the real thing just works.

Beyond the bench, I'm into 3D printing and fabrication, tabletop games, writing, skiing, and data visualization — almost always happier creating than consuming.
